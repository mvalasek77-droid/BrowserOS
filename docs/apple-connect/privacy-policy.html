Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

SteroidOS is an iOS browser companion for Apple Watch. The iPhone app loads web pages with WebKit and sends structured page data to the paired Apple Watch so the watch app can render content natively.

Data Processed by the App

SteroidOS may process URLs, page titles, page text, form metadata, media metadata, bookmarks, history, settings, and diagnostic logs on your devices. This information is used to provide browsing, watch handoff, bug reporting, and troubleshooting features.

Data Stored on Device

Bookmarks, history, settings, terms acceptance, and diagnostic logs are stored locally on your devices. Diagnostic logs are limited to recent app events and error messages.

Data Sent to the Paired Watch

When WatchConnectivity is active, SteroidOS sends structured page data, navigation state, load progress, and media metadata from the iPhone to the paired Apple Watch.

Third-Party Websites and Apps

Websites you visit may collect data under their own privacy policies. Some services may be opened in their official iOS apps or websites instead of being rendered in SteroidOS.

Bug Reports

If you choose to submit a bug report, the report may include details you type and recent diagnostic logs. Do not include passwords, payment details, private tokens, or sensitive personal information in a bug report.

Data Sharing

SteroidOS does not sell personal data. Bug reports are shared only when you choose to send them through GitHub, email, or another system-provided sharing path.

Contact

Questions can be sent to support@steroidos.app.
